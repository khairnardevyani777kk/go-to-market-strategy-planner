# Go-To-Market Strategy Planner for New Product Launch

## Overview
A practical Product Management and GTM case study for **LaunchPilot AI**, an AI-assisted SaaS platform for planning, launching and optimizing products.

## Business Problem
Launches fail when teams lack focused targeting, positioning, validated pricing, channel discipline, readiness and measurable optimization.

## Objective
Cover market sizing, segmentation, personas, positioning, pricing, channels, funnel, revenue, readiness, risk and 30-60-90 planning.

## Market
Indian B2B SaaS/technology/e-commerce SMB and mid-market.

## TAM-SAM-SOM
TAM ₹600 Cr | SAM ₹144 Cr | SOM ₹7.2 Cr

## Recommended GTM
Value-based subscription at ₹9,999/month starting price. Prioritize LinkedIn, webinars, email, SEO and partnerships.

## Funnel
Awareness → Interest → Lead → Qualified Lead → Demo/Trial → Conversion → Activation → Retention

## Launch Readiness
Target 80/100+ with no critical blocker.
