# GTM Analysis Report — LaunchPilot AI

## 1. Executive Summary
LaunchPilot AI is positioned as an integrated Go-To-Market planning and analytics workspace for B2B teams launching new products. The recommended entry segment is Indian SaaS, technology and e-commerce SMB/mid-market companies.

## 2. Market Opportunity
| Layer | Planning Estimate |
|---|---:|
| TAM | ₹600 Cr |
| SAM | ₹144 Cr |
| SOM | ₹7.2 Cr |

These figures are case-study assumptions for planning and portfolio demonstration.

## 3. Target Customer
Primary launch personas:
- Founder / Growth Lead
- Product Manager
- Product Marketing Manager
- Marketing Manager
- Business Analyst

The strongest initial fit is teams that have recurring launches, measurable digital acquisition and a need for cross-functional GTM alignment.

## 4. Positioning
For product, growth and marketing teams that struggle to coordinate market, customer, channel and revenue decisions, LaunchPilot AI is an integrated GTM planning workspace that converts fragmented launch planning into a measurable, data-driven workflow.

## 5. Value Proposition
**FOR** product, growth and marketing teams  
**WHO** need clearer customer, channel and revenue decisions  
**OUR PRODUCT** provides an integrated GTM planning and analytics workspace  
**UNLIKE** generic project-management or disconnected marketing tools  
**WE** connect segmentation, positioning, pricing, channels, funnel, forecast, readiness and risk.

## 6. Pricing Recommendation
Recommended starting point: **₹9,999/month**, supported by annual pricing and a low-friction trial. The strategy should be tested using value-based pricing experiments rather than simply copying competitors.

## 7. Channel Recommendation
Prioritize:
1. LinkedIn
2. Webinars and product demos
3. Email nurture
4. SEO/problem-led content
5. Partnerships

Google Ads, YouTube, community marketing, Meta and influencers should initially be treated as test channels.

## 8. Revenue Scenarios
- Worst Case: 500 leads, 6% conversion, 30 customers.
- Base Case: 1,200 leads, 10% conversion, 120 customers.
- Best Case: 2,000 leads, 15% conversion, 300 customers.

## 9. Launch Readiness
Use a cross-functional score across Product, Engineering, Sales, Marketing, Customer Support, Legal/Compliance and Operations. Recommended launch gate: **80/100+**, with no critical unresolved blocker.

## 10. Major Risks
Highest-priority risks are low market demand and high CAC. Other important risks include competitor response, weak positioning, low conversion, poor onboarding, product bugs and low retention.

## 11. 30-60-90 Recommendation
**Days 1–30:** Validate ICP, personas, pain points, pricing and product readiness.  
**Days 31–60:** Launch acquisition experiments, demos, trials, webinars and partnerships.  
**Days 61–90:** Optimize CAC, conversion, onboarding and retention; scale winning channels.

## 12. Final Strategic Decision
Proceed with a controlled pilot. Do not scale acquisition until customer demand, pricing, CAC, conversion, activation and retention show acceptable evidence. Scale the channels and segments that demonstrate repeatable economics.
